BAY WEB INDIA / LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains information handling for the Bay Web India informational website and enquiries made through its contact links. A separate written engagement may include additional confidentiality and data-processing terms.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, the information you choose to send may include your name, business role, organisation, email address, phone number, enquiry details and attachments. The website itself has no account registration, payment checkout or enquiry submission form. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or sensitive personal information in an initial enquiry.

Technical information and hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it collects visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes when a Pages site is visited. Hosting and communications providers may also process connection details and service records under their own policies. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

The current website code does not set cookies or use browser storage, advertising pixels, embedded third-party content or analytics scripts. It does not track visitors across third-party websites, and it does not change its behaviour in response to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals because those tracking activities are not implemented. Hosting-provider practices are described in the provider’s privacy statement. External email and WhatsApp links open services with their own privacy practices.

How information is used

Information received in an enquiry is used to respond, assess the requested assignment, prepare a scope, communicate about services you request, and maintain related business records. Information may also be used as needed to protect systems, prevent misuse, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations.

Disclosure and service providers

Information may be processed by hosting, email, telecommunications and other service providers used to handle your request. Disclosure may also be necessary to comply with law, protect legal rights, or involve an adviser or specialist as authorised for an engagement. This website has no mechanism for selling personal information or sharing it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

International handling

Bay Web India operates from Jaipur, India. Enquiries from the United States and other locations may be accessed and handled in India. Service providers may process information in other countries. Where applicable law requires additional transfer safeguards, these must be addressed before the relevant processing.

Retention and security

Enquiry and engagement records are retained for the purpose for which they were received and for applicable recordkeeping, legal or dispute-resolution needs. The appropriate period depends on the record and engagement. No website, email channel or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security. Confidential assignment materials should be exchanged through a method agreed in advance.

Your requests and choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you provided, or to ask about its handling. Additional rights may apply depending on your location and the applicable law. Requests may require identity verification, and some records may need to be retained for legal reasons. Any non-essential business communications can be declined by replying to the sender. We do not condition an enquiry on consent to marketing.

Children

This is a business-to-business website intended for adults. It does not solicit children’s information. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, contact us so it can be addressed.

Policy changes

Updates will appear on this page with a revised date. Material changes affecting information already provided will be communicated directly where applicable law requires it.
