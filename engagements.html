04 / WAYS TO ENGAGE

Start focused.
Go deeper where it matters.

Choose an initial review or a broader assessment. The final scope depends on your decision and the information available.

01 / INITIAL SCREEN

Company background review

For initial supplier or counterparty screening.

  • Entity status, incorporation and directors
  • Accessible public ownership and financial indicators
  • Business activity, website and address consistency
  • Preliminary litigation and adverse-media search
  • Concise red-flag summary
Discuss this review ↗
02 / FINANCIAL FOCUS

Financial red-flag review

For buyers and advisers considering material exposure.

  • Up to three years of supplied financial statements
  • Profitability, liquidity, leverage and cash-flow trends
  • Working-capital and earnings-quality indicators
  • Related-party indicators and feasible peer comparison
  • Financial red flags and management questions
Discuss this review ↗
03 / DEEPER ASSESSMENT

Commercial & financial review

For acquisitions, joint ventures and strategic partnerships.

  • Relevant elements of both preceding reviews
  • Industry, competitor and business-model assessment
  • Concentration risks, where data are provided
  • Management, promoter and operational research
  • Integrated risk matrix and specialist referral issues
Discuss this review ↗

Every engagement starts with a written scope. Target entity, documents, sources, exclusions, schedule and revisions are agreed upfront. Site visits, multi-entity reviews and specialist verification are scoped separately.

CLEAR BOUNDARIES / BETTER EXPECTATIONS

Know what the evidence can establish.

What we can document

  • Registered corporate data and accessible public filings
  • Supplied accounts and identifiable inconsistencies
  • Public litigation and adverse-media indicators
  • Documented commercial questions and risk factors

What needs further verification

  • Actual cash, inventory and liability balances
  • Authenticity of every underlying transaction
  • Absence of unrecorded disputes or contingent claims
  • On-site capacity, quality or performance

Written terms cover scope, reliance, limitations and confidentiality. Conflicts are assessed and client information is protected.
