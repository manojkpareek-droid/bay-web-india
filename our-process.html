03 / HOW WE WORK

Traceable sources.
Transparent judgement.

Each assignment begins with a written scope and ends with a source-documented, risk-ranked report. Assumptions and limitations remain visible throughout.

01

Scope

Define the decision, questions, records, exclusions and timetable in writing.

02

Verify

Check accessible sources and record the evidence in a dated source log.

03

Analyse

Test the financial and commercial picture; identify gaps and inconsistencies.

04

Report

Rank the risks and set out focused questions and next steps.

CLEAR BOUNDARIES / BETTER EXPECTATIONS

Know what the evidence can establish.

What we can document

  • Registered corporate data and accessible public filings
  • Supplied accounts and identifiable inconsistencies
  • Public litigation and adverse-media indicators
  • Documented commercial questions and risk factors

What needs further verification

  • Actual cash, inventory and liability balances
  • Authenticity of every underlying transaction
  • Absence of unrecorded disputes or contingent claims
  • On-site capacity, quality or performance
