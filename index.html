CLEAR BOUNDARIES / BETTER EXPECTATIONS

Know what the evidence can establish.

What we can document

  • Registered corporate data and accessible public filings
  • Supplied accounts and identifiable inconsistencies
  • Public litigation and adverse-media indicators
  • Documented commercial questions and risk factors

What needs further verification

  • Actual cash, inventory and liability balances
  • Authenticity of every underlying transaction
  • Absence of unrecorded disputes or contingent claims
  • On-site capacity, quality or performance

Written terms cover scope, reliance, limitations and confidentiality. Conflicts are assessed and client information is protected. These services provide factual corporate and commercial research; they do not constitute legal or tax advice, a statutory audit, or a securities buy, sell or hold recommendation.
