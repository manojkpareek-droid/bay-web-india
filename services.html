01 / WHAT WE DO

Six services.
One disciplined approach.

From first screening to detailed assessment, we turn available records into structured, decision-ready insight. Each engagement has an agreed scope and clear evidence boundaries.

01

Financial due diligence

Understand what the numbers reveal.

Review revenue quality, profitability, working capital, indebtedness, cash generation and unusual financial trends.

YOUR DELIVERABLE

Financial risk assessment, ratios, red flags and management queries.

02

Corporate & counterparty research

Know who is on the other side.

Review incorporation, directors, ownership, history, related-party indicators, litigation signals and business reputation.

YOUR DELIVERABLE

A documented company dossier and risk register.

03

Supplier & outsourcing assessment

Look beyond the quoted price.

Evaluate financial stability, dependency risks and the commercial credibility of manufacturing or service counterparties.

YOUR DELIVERABLE

Supplier comparison, risk scorecard and diligence questions.

04

Financial modelling & valuation support

Make assumptions visible.

Explore forecasts, cash-flow scenarios, working-capital models and preliminary valuation analyses for business decisions.

YOUR DELIVERABLE

Editable model, assumptions register and executive summary.

05

Healthcare & sector intelligence

Bring sector context to the decision.

Assess hospitals, diagnostics, insurers and health-tech businesses using sector-specific metrics and operating models.

YOUR DELIVERABLE

Benchmarking, market assessment and sector research report.

06

Research for professional firms

Extend your India research capacity.

Company profiles, competitor comparisons, target screens and India-specific briefing materials, supported under confidentiality terms.

YOUR DELIVERABLE

Research dossier, source log and management briefing.
